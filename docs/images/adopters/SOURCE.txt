Adopter logos supplied in the Knative Kiosk presentation, slide 13:
https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hWaz8r4D3Vrv2gRjNnoAvXNyEOKKvK3u5q2_-sNI87o/

The selected organizations and artwork follow that slide. Destinations were
checked against the Knative community adopter register linked from the slide:
https://github.com/knative/community/blob/main/ADOPTERS.MD

Artwork was extracted from the provided PDF. Empty canvas was cropped and
white-matted backgrounds, letter counters, and antialiased fringes were made
transparent. Intentional white artwork inside the Scaleway and Grey Rook
badges was retained. No logos were redrawn or intentionally recolored.

These files are sized specifically for the homepage's 160 by 64 CSS-pixel
logo slots. Default lossless WebP images fit within 160 by 64 pixels; @2x
variants fit within 320 by 128 pixels and are selected through srcset.
Grey Rook's supplied raster is only 165 by 33 pixels, so it has no artificially
upscaled @2x variant. Intrinsic aspect ratios are preserved throughout.

Logos remain the trademarks and artwork of their respective owners.
