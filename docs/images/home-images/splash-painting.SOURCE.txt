Original panoramic raster landscape made for the Knative website.
The user's Hockney references inform the long view and broad field composition;
no pixels, outlines, or painted details from an existing artwork are reproduced.
The artwork uses Knative-family blues, cyan, deep ink, and cool white.
A continuous, layered blue-teal forest covers the full rear hillside from edge
to edge. Overlapping tree silhouettes replace the narrow tree strip and isolated
foreground trees. Dark, restrained colors preserve contrast behind the headline;
the river and open foreground remain visible.
No Knative logo or watermark is included in the painting.
The upper sky is cropped to lift the landscape while retaining its bottom edge.
Kuack is rendered alongside the text as a separate responsive image layer from
../mascot/kuack.png; see ../mascot/SOURCE.txt for its provenance.
